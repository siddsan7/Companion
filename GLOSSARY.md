# Companion

Companion connects collected material to project ideas and proposed experiments.

## Language

**Capture**
Material collected for possible later use. Initial capture types are thought, link, and reel import.

**Imported Capture**
A capture whose source material comes from eligible validated Reel to MD output, whether or not it was manually downloaded.

**Annotation**
User commentary linked to a capture and owned independently of the imported source material.

**Project Brief**
A user-approved description of a project's current goals and constraints grounded in selected project documents. A conversation refers to a particular brief version.

**Saved Idea**
An interpretation or conclusion the user chooses to retain, together with its supporting references and uncertainty. It may draw on several captures.

**Experiment Proposal**
A saved idea that includes a testable hypothesis, a proposed test, and an observable result.
_Avoid_ treating a proposed experiment as an executed experiment or a verified outcome.

**Today**
A view of material first captured into Companion during the current calendar day in the PC's local timezone. Later source updates preserve the original capture instant, while timezone changes can alter day groupings.

**Source Revision**
A particular version of source material that an idea can refer to even after the current material changes.

**Revision Snapshot**
A preserved copy of an imported source revision's text, evidence references, provenance, and review information.

**Import Conflict**
An unresolved difference between a user's changes to an imported capture and an incoming source revision. Both versions remain available while updates to that capture are paused.

**Conversation**
A resumable exchange with the idea partner, including its messages and the source and project context used by each turn. A conversation can contain ideas that the user has not chosen to save.

**Context Package**
The question, allowed conversation history, and selected source and project excerpts included for a particular agent turn. Removing a source also excludes history and summaries derived from it from subsequent packages.

**Source Dependency**
A recorded relationship between material and the messages or summaries derived from it. Dependencies determine what remains eligible when source material is removed or rejected.

**Local History Archive**
A user-exported backup of conversations, application settings, and vault identity information. It is separate from a vault backup and excludes sign-in credentials.

**Reconciliation**
A check of selected source locations against known import outcomes to find eligible new or changed material.

**Re-import Suppression**
The user's instruction to keep a removed capture from returning through automatic intake until explicitly restored.

**Discovery Corpus**
The material eligible for automatic context discovery. It includes Companion-managed captures, annotations, saved ideas, approved briefs, and other notes explicitly included by the user.

**Review Decision**
A recorded human judgment about source material, such as acceptance, editing, or rejection. It is distinct from processing completeness and extraction uncertainty.

**Comparison Fixture**
Authored synthetic source material with inspectable passages and known limitations, used to compare reasoning methods. It is identified separately from real imported material.

**Catch-up Check**
One reconciliation for accumulated changes after a scheduled check was missed. It preserves the original recurring schedule.

**Held Import**
An incoming artifact that cannot yet be admitted because its identity, structure, or reviewed-revision precedence needs resolution.

**User-supplied Excerpt**
Text the user explicitly associates with a source link. Its attribution records the user's input and does not imply independent checking of the webpage.

**No Relevant Context**
A retrieval outcome in which no suitable source material was found for the request.

**No Supported Connection**
A reasoning outcome in which the available material supports no useful transfer to the project.
