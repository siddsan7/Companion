# Separate imported source ownership from user writing

Re-imports refresh one current imported capture per logical export. User annotations and saved ideas remain separate linked notes, accepting extra navigation for predictable ownership. Every accepted imported revision has an immutable text-and-metadata snapshot in the vault; original videos are referenced through available locators. External edits pause updates to the affected capture and preserve local and incoming content for explicit resolution.

Retention has no automatic expiry. Capture deletion defaults to current content plus all retained revisions after a dependency preview. Independent writing remains with missing-reference warnings and may retain quotations. Further removal needs separate explicit scope. Confirmed removal suppresses automatic re-import until restoration. Missing notes, verified moves, identity collisions and inaccessible vaults follow the explicit repair policy rather than silent recreation or mass deletion.

These choices accept storage growth, occasional repair work and update interruptions to preserve evidence and user writing.
